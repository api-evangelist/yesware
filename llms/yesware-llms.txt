# Yesware
> Yesware is a sales productivity platform that provides email tracking, automated outreach, and Salesforce integration for Gmail and Outlook.

Yesware helps high-performing sales teams close more deals by providing real-time engagement data and automating repetitive tasks directly within the inbox.

## Core Features
- [Email Tracking](https://www.yesware.com/features/email-tracking/): Real-time notifications for opens, link clicks, and attachment views.
- [Multi-Channel Campaigns](https://www.yesware.com/features/campaigns/): Automated email sequences with integrated phone and LinkedIn tasks.
- [Meeting Scheduler](https://www.yesware.com/features/meeting-scheduler/): Personalized booking links that sync with O365 and Google.
- [Salesforce Sync](https://www.yesware.com/features/salesforce-integration/): Bi-directional data sync for logging activities and attachments.
- [Reporting & Analytics](https://www.yesware.com/features/reporting/): Performance dashboards for template success and team activity.
